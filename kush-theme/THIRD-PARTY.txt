Adapted key/CPS boxes and MouseHandler callback into existing Kush modules.
Kush uses monotonic one-second windows on the game thread rather than an extra timer thread.
Armor Status is the Kush native module, extended with held items and durability bars.

TwelveMonkeys ImageIO 3.12.0, BSD-3-Clause, official WebP thumbnail decoder.
Licenses included in the distributed JAR under licenses/.

Cosmetica catalog content is fetched from its public API/CDN.
Cosmetics remain local previews and equipment. No shared player outfit service is added.
